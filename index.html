About

Project Management
Specialist

I enjoy helping people work better together. I’m at my best when a project has moving parts because my strengths are listening to what the team needs, bringing structure to the work, and keeping everyone informed as plans move forward.

At SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, I worked with engineering, IT, procurement, finance, and engineering teams on projects ranging from applications/tools impletenation to documentation compliance. One project I’m especially proud of helped reduce physical documentation by about 70% and made information easier for the team to access. As an independent consultant, I also designed a lead-management system used by more than 100 people across 76 organizations.

I’m looking to bring that experience to a project management specialist role in tech. I’m drawn to work that connects people and technology, especially when a new tool or way of working can make someone’s day easier.

Outside of work, I volunteer as a Spanish interpreter with the Justice & Diversity Center in San Francisco. It’s a meaningful way for me to use my skills to support my community.

I’d love to connect with teams looking for someone who can bring clarity, care, and steady follow-through to their projects!
